Abstract

We introduce YT-SarcasmVis, the first large-scale YouTube multimodal dataset for visual sarcasm detection, comprising 18,432 annotated video clips across six thematic categories. Each clip is annotated with seven independent layers including binary sarcasm label, irony subtype, intensity, VAD sentiment, visual focus regions, temporal spans, and modality attribution. We further propose TCASN, achieving 73.4% F1.
